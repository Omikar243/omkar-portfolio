About me

A little curious,
a lot rigorous.

I’m an AI/ML engineer and data scientist from Mumbai. I build things that turn a complicated question into something people can actually use.

Who I am

I work at Catalysk, where I build AI for sustainable finance. My foundation is in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, but my approach is practical: understand the problem, question the data, and keep working until the system holds up outside a notebook.

What I build

I build retrieval pipelines, agentic decision systems and production ML services that connect rules, evidence and reasoning. I also like taking an idea all the way to a product: from an AI-powered fashion platform with 3D previews to a medical-imaging model with a literature-grounded assistant.

What I’m working toward

My ambition is to build AI that earns its place in everyday life — useful enough to solve a real problem, transparent enough to question, and dependable enough to trust. I want to keep growing from someone who builds models into someone who builds thoughtful, end-to-end intelligent products.
