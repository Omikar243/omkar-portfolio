

## Final playground stabilization

The last scroll defect came from two competing interactions: a hard canvas touch boundary and perspective tilt continuing while the page moved. The canvas now allows vertical pan, the article no longer carries a Lenis exclusion, orbit tilt is suspended for the duration of a scroll burst, and the game boots near the viewport boundary. Meaningful spotlight movement can produce a short opt-in cue through the existing ambient graph.


## Pitch Deck Mode refresh

Pitch Deck Mode is a presentation layer over the existing site, not a replacement theme. A persistent toggle enables a chapter rail with thesis, operator, evidence, proof, lab, toolkit and invitation. Desktop chapters use full-viewport pacing and proximity snap; mobile keeps native scrolling and uses a compact bottom rail. The mode persists locally and can be exited with Escape. The walkthrough video is recut so the first 20 seconds communicate the portfolio thesis and visual signature before moving into the deeper sections.
